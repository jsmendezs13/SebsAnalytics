The system

One system for your guests, your team and your dining room

01

For your guests

A guest who rates a dish already has an account, so booking takes three taps, and every booking comes with its own conversation.

  • Website on your domain
  • Food and drinks menus with pairings
  • Fork’s Rating on every dish
  • Reservations, rooms, big parties
  • Guest messages, photos and PDFs
  • Rewards: Lucky Bite, stamps, invites

02

For your team

Your team’s app on the home screen, without the App Store. Each person has their own login and sees only what their role allows.

  • Notifications for messages, big parties, new bookings and cancellations
  • Every change to prices, hours, menu or team: who, what and when
  • A text code on each new phone or computer, trusted for 60 days
  • Your numbers: tables, people, arrivals, no-shows and cancellations

03

In your dining room

Wooden tap menus, engraved for your restaurant. A tap of the phone opens the full menu, with a QR code as backup. Less paper, and no reprints when a price changes.

Research

Menu-price research and economic analysis

Seb’s Analytics is also an economics practice. With the menus we run, we follow how New York menu prices change and what diners choose.

  1. 1Menu prices over time. Every price change on our menus is recorded, dish by dish and month by month.
  2. 2What diners choose. Ratings and bookings by dish, in anonymous groups of 10 or more, by age group.
  3. 3Analysis for your business. Pricing questions for your restaurant, or a market study for your company.
