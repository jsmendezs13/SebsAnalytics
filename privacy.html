Privacy policy

Effective November 10, 2026

This policy covers sebsanalytics.com, the website of Seb's Analytics LLC, a New York company. It explains what we keep when you book a demo, why, and for how long. The websites we build for restaurants have their own privacy policies.

What we keep when you book a demo

  • Your name, your restaurant or company, your email address, your phone number, and the address of the restaurant where we meet for the demo.
  • The day and time you picked, and when you booked.
  • A one-way fingerprint of your internet address. We use it only to stop someone from booking the whole calendar; we don't keep the address itself.

We use these details only to hold your demo, to contact you about it, and to keep the calendar free of abuse. We don't sell them, we don't share them for advertising, and we don't add you to a mailing list.

Emails

When you book, we email you the day and time with a calendar file, and we email ourselves that a demo was booked. If we have to cancel, we email you. Replies come to advisory@sebsanalytics.com.

Who helps us

  • Vercel hosts the website.
  • Supabase stores the demo bookings.
  • Resend sends the emails.
  • Photon (by komoot) suggests addresses while you type in the address box, from OpenStreetMap data. What you type there goes to it through our server, so it doesn't see your internet address.

They handle your details only to provide these services to us. The website loads no advertising, no tracking tools and no outside fonts.

Cookies

Visitors get no cookies. Our own staff page uses one sign-in cookie, only for the people of Seb's Analytics who sign in.

How long we keep it

A demo booking is deleted 2 years after the day of the demo. Copies of the emails can stay longer in the mailboxes that received them and, for a limited time, in Resend's records.

Your choices

Write to advisory@sebsanalytics.com to see, correct or delete what we keep about you, or to cancel a demo. We answer within 30 days.

Children

This website is for restaurant owners and businesses. It is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly keep their details.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update it here and change the date at the top.
